# Pre-Launch Checklist

## Business fundamentals
- [ ] Finalize business name and confirm it's not trademarked (search CIPO's trademark database, and a general web/social handle check)
- [ ] Register your business (sole proprietorship, or incorporate — a CPA or lawyer can advise which fits your plan)
- [ ] Apply for a GST/HST number once you're at or expect to exceed $30,000 in revenue (or voluntarily earlier, if it helps with input tax credits)
- [ ] Get professional liability (errors & omissions) insurance — standard for bookkeeping/accounting services
- [ ] Finish your CPA designation requirements, or confirm what you can call yourself/advertise before it's complete (provincial CPA bodies have specific rules about using "CPA," "accountant," etc. before certification)
- [ ] Open a separate business bank account

## Domain & email
- [ ] Register your `.ca` (and ideally matching `.com`) domain through a CIRA-accredited registrar
- [ ] Set up professional business email on your domain (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365) — do not launch with a Gmail/Hotmail address, it undercuts the trust you're building
- [ ] Set up SPF/DKIM/DMARC records so your emails don't land in spam

## Website
- [ ] Replace every placeholder (business name, email, phone, pricing) — see README.md "Before you deploy"
- [ ] Connect the contact form to a real backend (Formspree, Resend, or a server action) so submissions actually reach you
- [ ] Add a real logo and favicon
- [ ] Set up Google Search Console and submit the sitemap
- [ ] Set up Google Analytics (or a privacy-friendly alternative like Plausible)
- [ ] Create and verify a Google Business Profile (helps local/Canadian SEO even for a virtual business — use a service-area business listing, not a public address)
- [ ] Add a privacy policy page (required if you're collecting form submissions and given you handle sensitive financial data) and terms of service
- [ ] Test the site on mobile, and with a screen reader or keyboard-only navigation

## Tools & software
- [ ] Set up your own QuickBooks Online / Xero / Wave accountant or partner account
- [ ] Choose a secure file-sharing tool for receiving client documents (e.g., a client portal, or an encrypted file-transfer tool — avoid plain email attachments for sensitive documents)
- [ ] Set up a scheduling tool (Calendly or similar) for the free consultation CTA, or confirm your form-to-calendar workflow

## Legal & compliance
- [ ] Confirm engagement letter / contract template for new clients (a lawyer or CPA-adjacent template service can help)
- [ ] Confirm your data retention and confidentiality practices are documented, even informally
- [ ] Understand PIPEDA (Canada's private-sector privacy law) obligations, since you'll be handling clients' financial information

## Marketing
- [ ] Set up LinkedIn and/or Instagram business profiles once you're ready to be more public-facing (can stay separate from your personal profile)
- [ ] Draft your first few pieces of content for a future blog (once added) — FAQs from real client questions are a good starting source
- [ ] Ask early clients for permission to use anonymized testimonials once you have them — do not publish invented ones
